Privacy policy

How Catoira Consulting Inc handles personal information for MVP for Jira and this website. Effective November 12, 2026.

In short: the app runs inside Atlassian’s cloud and stores your map layout, not your issue content. We can’t see your Jira site. We only handle the little information that reaches us through support emails, Atlassian’s Marketplace licensing records, and app error logs.

Who we are

MVP for Jira (“the app”) and this website are provided by Catoira Consulting Inc, a British Columbia company (“we”, “us”). Questions about this policy: support@catoira.app.

Information in the app

The app is built on Atlassian Forge and runs entirely in Atlassian’s cloud. It stores, in Atlassian’s Forge hosted storage for your Jira site:

  • MVP titles, step and lane titles, linked release ids, and the Jira project id;
  • the Jira issue ids on each MVP and where they sit on the map;
  • the Atlassian account id of the person who created each MVP, used to decide who may edit it;
  • the site settings chosen by a Jira admin.

It does not store issue content (summaries, descriptions, comments, attachments), or anyone’s name, email address or avatar. Issue details are read from Jira when a map is shown, as the person viewing it, and are not kept.

We do not have access to this stored data or to your Jira site. Your organization controls it: it can delete MVPs, delete all app data from the app’s settings page, or uninstall the app. For this data, your organization’s agreement with Atlassian and Atlassian’s policies apply to how it is hosted. Details are on our Security page.

The app changes Jira data only when a person acts in it: story points when edited on a card, and fix versions when cards move between lanes linked to releases (if your Jira admin leaves that setting on). These changes are made as that person, under their Jira permissions.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads no third-party fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log basic technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, under GitHub’s own privacy statement.

How long we keep information

  • Support emails: for as long as needed to help you, then up to two years for our records, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.
  • License details: for as long as your organization has a license, and afterwards as needed for tax, accounting and legal obligations.
  • App error logs: kept by Atlassian for a limited period, after which they are removed.
  • Data stored by the app: until your organization deletes it or uninstalls the app, as described on our Security page.

Where information is processed

We are based in Canada. Information we receive may be processed in Canada and in other countries where our service providers operate. App data stays in Atlassian’s cloud and follows your organization’s Atlassian data residency settings.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or limit how we use it, and to complain to your data protection authority. To make a request, email support@catoira.app. We will respond within the time the law requires. For data stored by the app in your Jira site, contact your organization’s Jira admin, who controls it.

Security

We protect the information we hold with reasonable safeguards, and the app follows Atlassian’s security requirements for Marketplace apps. See our Security page.

Children

The app and this website are for businesses and are not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell license contacts by email before the change takes effect.
