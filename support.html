Support

Questions, problems or ideas: write to us and a person who builds the app will answer.

Contact us

Email support@catoira.app. We answer on business days (Monday to Friday, Pacific time) and aim to reply within two business days.

To help us answer quickly, include:

  • your Jira site address (for example, yourcompany.atlassian.net);
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • a screenshot if something looks wrong, and your browser.

Please don’t send passwords, API tokens, or confidential issue content.

Before you write

  • Can’t find the MVP tab? Look under More at the end of the project’s tabs, and drag it onto the tab row to keep it there.
  • A button is missing or greyed out? It usually depends on your Jira permissions or the site’s plan. See Who can do what and Plans and limited mode.
  • A card shows a warning after moving lanes? Jira couldn’t update its fix version. The warning shows Jira’s reason. See Releases and fix versions.

Full answers are in the docs.

Security reports

Found a security issue? Email support@catoira.app with “Security report” in the subject. See how we handle reports.

Billing and licenses

Subscriptions, invoices, refunds and trials are handled by Atlassian through the Marketplace. Your Jira site admin can manage them in Atlassian Administration. For anything about the app itself, write to us.
