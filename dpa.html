Data Processing Addendum

The data protection terms for MVP for Jira. This addendum is an Attachment to the end user agreement. Effective November 12, 2026.

1. Scope and roles

This Data Processing Addendum (“DPA”) forms part of the end user agreement between the customer (“Customer”) and Catoira Consulting Inc, a British Columbia company (“Provider”), for MVP for Jira (the “Product”). It applies to personal data that the Product processes for Customer (“Customer Personal Data”).

Customer is the controller of Customer Personal Data. Provider is a processor acting on Customer’s behalf. Atlassian, which hosts the Product on its Forge platform, is Provider’s subprocessor.

2. Details of processing

Subject matter and purposeProviding the Product: showing Jira issues on story maps, storing map layouts and settings, and deciding who may edit each MVP.
Nature of processingStoring, reading, displaying and deleting data within Atlassian’s cloud.
Data subjectsCustomer’s Jira users who use the Product.
Categories of personal dataAtlassian account IDs of users who create MVPs (stored). Issue details, including assignee names, are read from Jira and shown to the viewing user without being stored by the Product.
Special categoriesNone intended.
DurationFor the term of the agreement, until the data is deleted as described in section 7.

3. Processing on instructions

Provider processes Customer Personal Data only to provide the Product, as set out in the agreement and as configured by Customer, unless the law requires otherwise; in that case Provider will tell Customer first if the law allows. The Product is designed so that Provider has no access to data stored by the Product in Customer’s site.

4. Confidentiality and security

Provider ensures that anyone it authorizes to process Customer Personal Data is bound by confidentiality. Provider maintains the technical and organizational measures described on the Security page, including running only on Atlassian’s platform, making Jira requests as the signed-in user, checking permissions on every action, and requesting only the permissions the Product needs.

5. Subprocessors

Customer authorizes the following subprocessor:

SubprocessorPurposeLocation
Atlassian (Forge platform)Hosting the Product’s code and stored dataFollows Customer’s Atlassian data residency settings

Provider will give at least 30 days’ notice to license contacts before adding a subprocessor. Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds; if the parties can’t resolve the objection, Customer may stop using the Product and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the unused period, through Atlassian.

6. Assistance and data subject requests

  • Provider reports stored Atlassian account IDs to Atlassian’s personal data reporting service and removes account IDs of closed accounts, as Atlassian requires.
  • Customer can delete data at any time: individual MVPs, issues on an MVP, or everything with Delete all app data on the settings page.
  • Provider will reasonably help Customer respond to data subject requests and with data protection impact assessments, to the extent Customer can’t do so with the Product’s own features.

7. Deletion and return

When the agreement ends or Customer uninstalls the Product, stored data is deleted under Atlassian’s data lifecycle for Forge apps, described on the Security page. Customer can delete everything immediately before uninstalling with Delete all app data. The Product stores no Customer content that needs returning; Jira issues stay in Jira.

8. Security incidents

Provider will notify Customer without undue delay, and within 72 hours, after becoming aware of a breach affecting Customer Personal Data, with the information Customer reasonably needs to meet its own obligations, and will take reasonable steps to contain it.

9. Information and audits

Provider will answer reasonable written questions about its compliance with this DPA, no more than once a year unless required after an incident or by a regulator.

10. International transfers

Stored data stays in Atlassian’s cloud under Customer’s data residency settings. Provider is in Canada, which the European Commission recognizes as providing adequate protection for commercial organizations. To the extent a transfer requires additional safeguards, the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (Module 2, controller to processor), and the UK addendum where relevant, are incorporated by reference.

11. General

This DPA is governed by the governing law of the agreement unless data protection law requires otherwise. If this DPA conflicts with the agreement on data protection matters, this DPA controls. Contact: support@catoira.app.
